## 3.1 What you will do

The Furusato model has 10 entity types, 72 static properties plus one time-series property, and 15 relationships. In this chapter you check why each part has its shape, so that you do not mix up meanings when questions arrive.

The distributed data-dictionary.txt is a design document generated from the actual CSV files and definitions. It is not an answer key for individual questions.

Before you start

- Have your sketch from Chapter 1 and the tables in Appendix A ready.
- Open data-dictionary.txt in the attachments folder of the distribution (optional).
