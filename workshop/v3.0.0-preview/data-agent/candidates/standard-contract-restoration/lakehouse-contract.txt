## Standard workshop contracts (highest priority)
- A prefecture name without a received/resident qualifier: return BOTH readings from dbo.ot_prefecture in one query: PrefReceivedStaticCount/PrefReceivedTotalYen (received by municipalities in that prefecture) and PrefResidentStaticCount/PrefResidentTotalYen (made by residents), with PrefectureId and PrefectureName. Never add them.
- UNFILTERED popularity or "the most" questions: rank by count AND by amount nationwide. Municipalities: agent_municipality_snapshot with ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY DonationCount DESC, MunicipalityId) AS CountOrdinal and ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY AmountYen DESC, MunicipalityId) AS AmountOrdinal. Gift categories: dbo.ot_gift_category with CategoryStaticCount and CategoryStaticTotalYen.
- ANY payment, category, date or geography filter: aggregate agent_donation_detail, which contains DonationPaymentMethod, CategoryId/CategoryName and the JST date columns. Never use agent_municipality_snapshot for a filtered ranking and never report those columns as unavailable.
- A MunicipalityId from operational observations: read MunicipalityId, MunicipalityName, DonationCount, AmountYen and the nationwide count/amount ranks from agent_municipality_snapshot with no date predicate. Do NOT select PrefectureId or PrefectureName for this observed-versus-static comparison: the Ontology source answers the prefecture through the forward MunicipalityInPrefecture relationship.
- Exact donation trace: agent_donation_detail WHERE DonationId=<id> with DonationAmountYen, donor/residence, recipient/prefecture, GiftId/GiftName and CategoryId/CategoryName; then every supplier from agent_supplier_catalog WHERE GiftId=<that GiftId>.
- Prefecture membership counts belong to Ontology unless the user explicitly says 名簿, seed or Lakehouse.
