## 10.2 Steps

12. After saving, PrefectureId may appear as a property of Municipality. It is a relationship key column, so leave it in place.
13. When the ontology update has finished, ask the Ontology agent (Plan) the following and confirm that 452025 returns 331 observations and JPY 5,737,000. Check the executed query it shows, and compare it with a KQL count and total under the same conditions.

```text
Stay in Plan. For the time-series property IncomingDonationAmountYen bound to Municipality with MunicipalityId 452025, total the observations from 2026-08-01 00:00:00 UTC (inclusive) to 2026-09-01 00:00:00 UTC (exclusive), and return the observation count and the total amount. State that the values include duplicates, and show the query you ran.
```

> Because Municipality uses two tables (Eventhouse and Lakehouse) after you add the time series, it may become Ineligible for a graph. This is not a binding failure (Chapter 16).

> Cut dates by UTC DonatedAt. If you cut by Japan time, rows on 1 September appear; that is expected.

> If the main ontology time-series binding is unavailable on screen, or saving or native querying fails, record the target and error and stop that feature. Ordinary KQL success does not establish time-series binding success; do not create another ontology.
