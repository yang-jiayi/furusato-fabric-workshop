6. Start a new conversation in the Ontology agent, ask an ambiguous question such as the following, and check whether the rule and descriptions were used in the answer. Replace &lt;GiftId&gt; with a GiftId from ot_gift. A rule does not force how SQL or KQL is generated and does not replace access control.

```text
Which supplier actually shipped the gift with GiftId <GiftId>?
```

7. (When you use the automated deployment in Chapter 24) For the ontology created by the automated deployment, Notebook 02 can preview and apply the statements of the four rules in one go. Run it with ACTION left as preview-metadata to review the changes, and apply (apply-metadata) only after the instructor confirms. See Chapter 24 and Appendix B for the parameters. It cannot be used on a manually built ontology, on an ontology with Metrics, or after handoff-ontology has run.

> Ontology Rules do not run notifications or actions as an Activator does, and the Activator in Chapter 9 is not created from Rules.

> With the current product behavior, ask_ontology over MCP does not use Rules in its answers (Chapter 20).
