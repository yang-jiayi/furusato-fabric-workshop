## 18.1 What you will do

Handle the same business terms with SQL, KQL, DAX and GQL and with the two AI experiences (Data Agent and Ontology agent), and compare the basis and limits of each result. Numbers that look alike are different values if their grain or scope differs.

Start each scenario in a new conversation. Keep the configuration, data and period fixed, and do not assume that unfinished graphs or Metrics are available.

Before you start

- Have the Data Agent from Chapter 17, the main ontology, the Chapter 16 graph when available, and a KQL queryset ready. Record unconfigured Metrics or time series separately from exercise prerequisites.
